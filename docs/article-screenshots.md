# Article Screenshots

## Common Article Rules

- Every article and guide ends with the shared ArticleSocialFooter, including its no-JavaScript HTML version. Use the shared Hopscup Crew banner and Telegram, YouTube and site links. Do not duplicate the footer in article content.
- Do not add automatic related-article recommendation cards without the author's explicit request.
- On desktop, keep the article table of contents sticky below the header. Long tables of contents scroll within the available viewport height; do not add one to mobile layouts.
- The proxy guide previews a checklist with its catalogue CTA followed directly by a compact footer. Its FAQ and related cards are removed. Do not roll this layout out to other articles until approved; the author will supply each checklist.
- Keep the author's wording and paragraph order. Do not invent or rewrite claims. Across the entire site, do not use em dashes in visible text: use a colon, comma or ordinary hyphen instead.
- When an article is edited, synchronize its React/static H1 and full content, Article dates and any existing FAQ schema. Do not migrate every article at once for these changes.
- When editing service sections, retain sponsored attribution for affiliate links, add available promo-code copying and the relevant internal catalogue link. Do not invent promo codes or discounts.

## Screenshot Rules

- Wrap article screenshots and covers in ArticleImageLink so they enlarge in the shared in-page viewer. Keep service logos and social banners as their original navigation links.
- Use the author's original screenshots. Do not recreate software interfaces or credentials with generative images.
- If only a fully redacted copy exists, ask for the original. Do not guess missing text.
- Keep masks already applied by the author unchanged.
- Apply additional masks only to sensitive values. Leave their first and last characters visible so the data format remains understandable.
- Use narrow opaque rectangles or replacement symbols, not broad blur. Keep separators, labels, buttons, protocol names and test results readable.
- Crop to the relevant interface area and retain enough context to understand the action.
- Export lightweight assets. Keep an unmodified original outside the public assets directory.
- Label invented example credentials explicitly as invented. Never represent invented credentials as actual account data.
